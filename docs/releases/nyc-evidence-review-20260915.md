## Priority 1.1 rules

This is research/commercial follow-up priority, not health risk, compliance certification, causation or purchase probability. Weights are transparent policy choices, not empirically calibrated predictions.

- Inspection findings: 40 plus 10 for exact Critical/PHH severity. Factor 1 through 90 days, 0.6 through 180, 0.3 through 365; older/unknown/future records do not add findings points. Use the strongest eligible inspection, not repeated citation totals.
- Exact-ticket published dismissals exclude only the matching citation. Paid fines do not establish physical remediation.
- Named-building official positive-test/remediation follow-up: 70 through 14 days, 45 through 30, 20 through 90. Closed episodes, culture-negative results, cleaning-complete-only records, undated records and area-only associations receive no notice points. Use the maximum of notice and inspection evidence, not their sum.
- Public sample follow-up: 18 for no usable public date; 20/25/30 for more than 36/45/60 days since a published sample. The first five days after day 31 are a reporting allowance, not proof of noncompliance. Operating status is not assumed. Future/invalid dates get no gap points and a review note.
- Recent inspection activity: 10 within 90 days only when not already represented by findings/notice evidence.
- Recent explicit cooling-tower DOB NOW project: at most 15 for a published lifecycle date within 90 days without a sign-off, cancellation, withdrawal or disapproval. Generic boiler/mechanical filings do not qualify.
- Registered equipment scale: six per additional unit, at most 18; this is commercial scale, not adverse evidence.
- Overall maximum 100. Applied component points sum exactly to the displayed score, including capping.

HPD housing/water violations, SWO dispositions, FISP/Local Law 11, domestic-water inspections/self-reports, 311 complaints, water benchmarks, CMS facilities, lead service lines, distribution quality, ACRIS, contacts and procurement remain separately visible context. They are reviewed but not automatically treated as cooling-tower faults. Local Law 11 filings are not Labor Law litigation filings.

Each account retains its previous scoring object. The full before/after report and validator cover all current accounts, not a sample. Underlying identifiers, inspections, sampling dates, regulatory records and history are preserved.
